The Project

Real is a modern real estate brokerage that needed a recruitment landing page to attract high-performing agents who were frustrated with their current brokerage — poor commission splits, monthly fees, lack of training, and no technology support.

The page needed to speak directly to ambitious agents ready to become top producers, clearly present Real's three core value propositions, and drive consultation bookings through multiple strategically placed CTAs.

I built the full page inside GoHighLevel — clean white design with cyan accents, luxury property photography, agent training stats, three value proposition sections with alternating image layouts, and a video section showcasing the stock rewards programme.

What I Built

🏠 Hero Section

"Ready To Become A Top Producing Agent?" — bold headline with luxury property background, pain-point subtext ("Don't be held back by commission splits and lack of support"), and a prominent Schedule Your Consult CTA.

📖 Unlocking Realtor Success

Narrative section positioning Real as the solution — explaining why agents fail at other brokerages, and how Real's network, training, and commission structure creates top producers consistently.

📊 Agent Training Stats Bar

Three bold numbers: 114 mastermind discussion replays, 20+ live interactive classes per week, and 350+ hours of on-demand training — establishing Real Academy as a serious competitive advantage.

💰 Value Prop 1: Industry Leading Splits

85%/15% commission split with a $12,000 cap, no monthly fees, keep 100% after capping. Presented with a luxury property image and a direct Schedule Your Consult button.

💻 Value Prop 2: State-of-the-Art Technology

Leverage tools to manage deals, stay connected, and receive support — positioned alongside a second luxury property visual. Tech advantage framed as a productivity and income multiplier.

📈 Value Prop 3: Pre-Cap Stock Purchasing Plan + Bonus

Agents can invest up to 5% of pre-cap commission in company stock, plus a 15% bonus at anniversary — making Real feel like a long-term wealth-building partner, not just a brokerage.

🎬 Video Section

"What Makes it Special? Watch Video" — embedded video showcasing the stock awards programme with a bold overlay: "Earn stock awards for capping and attracting agents." Drives emotional connection.

🎯 Multiple Schedule CTAs

Seven strategically placed "Schedule Your Consult" buttons throughout the page — ensuring no agent has to scroll far to take action once they're convinced.
