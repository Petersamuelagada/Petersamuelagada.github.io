The project

Make the event feel worth registering for.

Roventa 2025 — a global conference on creativity, tech, and business — needed a complete event landing page to drive ticket registrations. The page needed to convey credibility, excitement, and urgency while making it easy for attendees to choose a ticket and register.

The goal was a fully conversion-optimised event page inside GoHighLevel with speaker profiles, a three-day schedule, sponsor logos, tiered ticket pricing, and testimonials to drive sign-ups.

The supplied build packages the event’s agenda, authority, community energy, price choices, and registration action into one clear attendee journey.
