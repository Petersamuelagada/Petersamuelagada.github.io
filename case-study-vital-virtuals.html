The project

Make a complex healthcare staffing offer easy to understand quickly.

Vital Virtuals needed a website that could clearly communicate a complex healthcare staffing offering to busy doctors and practice managers — people who have no time to read lengthy pages and need to immediately understand the ROI of hiring a virtual scribe or assistant.

The key challenge was differentiating Vital Virtuals’ live virtual scribes from AI scribes — making the case that human scribes working in real time are superior for clinical accuracy, EMR integration, and provider satisfaction.

I built the full site inside GoHighLevel — yellow and white clean design, side-by-side AI vs live scribe comparison, 4 VA service types, staffing solution breakdown, pricing plans, and a discovery call booking form.

What the page needed to do

Move from a clear documentation-time outcome into the operational and clinical differences that matter to a practice manager, before showing service choice and a booking path.

What I built

A healthcare staffing page based on clarity, comparison, and operational relief.

The original GoHighLevel website puts a single headline result upfront, then supplies the clinical and staffing proof necessary to turn a time-poor decision-maker into a discovery-call lead.

01 · BOOKING FIRST

Hero with Booking Form

“Reduce Documentation Time by 70%” — bold result-led headline with an inline Book Discovery Call form in the hero, removing friction and capturing leads immediately on page load.

02 · CLINICAL COMPARISON

AI Scribe vs Live Virtual Scribe

Side-by-side clinical performance showdown — First-Pass Note Accuracy, Provider Editing Time, EMR Integration, Chart Review, and more. Live scribes win on every clinical metric.

03 · SERVICE RATIONALE

Why Virtual Live Medical Scribes Are Superior

4-pillar breakdown: Real-Time Context-Aware Documentation, Direct EMR Entry with No Post-Visit Edits, Adaptive & Customisable to Charting Style, and HIPAA-Compliant US-Managed Infrastructure.

04 · STAFFING SCOPE

Other VA Services

4 additional service types with photos: Dental Receptionist, Medical Receptionist, Patient Intake Coordinator, and Medical Biller & Coder — positioned as a one-stop healthcare staffing solution.

05 · PRACTICE PAINS

Built for Busy Healthcare Businesses

Pain point section addressing exactly what practice managers deal with: documentation overload, staff turnover, billing errors, and compliance headaches — all solved by Vital Virtuals.

06 · STAFFING CHOICE

Staffing Solutions & Pricing

Full staffing package breakdown: Access to Tier Tracking, Weekly Billing, Dedicated Account Manager, 100% Flat Rate, No Setup Fees. Pricing table: Full Time / Part Time × English / Bilingual VAs.

07 · ANSWER LAYER

FAQ Section

Key questions addressed: difference between AI and live scribes, how scribes differ across specialties, workflow for excellent scribe results, and whether scribes help with entering orders or medications.

08 · BRAND COMPLETENESS

Social Media Footer

Full branded footer with social media links, services list, contact details, and address — giving the brand a complete professional web presence.

Tools & integrations
