The project

Performance data turned into useful coaching context.

Coaching teams managing multiple athletes face a constant challenge — tracking individual performance over time, spotting when an athlete needs attention, and generating consistent weekly reports without spending hours on manual data work.

This n8n automation handles the entire pipeline — from logging a training session to delivering a full weekly performance summary to the coaching team via Slack and email. OpenAI GPT acts as the analysis engine, interpreting performance data against historical records and generating actionable insights automatically.

The system stores training records, combines current and historical data, analyses the trend, alerts the team when thresholds are breached, and produces weekly athlete-by-athlete summaries.
Workflow evidence · data ingestion, AI analysis, threshold checking, and weekly reporting

The 4 automation phases

From session entry to weekly visibility.

The original project is structured around four connected phases that give coaches current context, immediate alerts, and consistent reporting.

PHASE 01

Fetch & combine historical data

When a training session form is submitted, the workflow stores the new record in Google Sheets, then fetches all prior historical records for that athlete. Current and historical data are combined into one dataset.

PHASE 02

Performance Analysis Agent

An OpenAI-powered Performance Analysis Agent processes the combined data — identifying trends, comparing session metrics, and generating written insights. Results are written back to Google Sheets automatically.

PHASE 03

Check performance threshold

A threshold check evaluates whether the athlete's metrics breach predefined limits. Breaches send an immediate Slack alert; otherwise, a standard email alert is sent.

PHASE 04

Weekly summary & distribution

A scheduled trigger fetches weekly training data, groups records by athlete, and passes each group to a Weekly Summary Agent. Summaries are sent to Slack and email automatically.
