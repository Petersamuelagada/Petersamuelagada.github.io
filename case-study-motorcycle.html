The project

A free guide needs to feel like the right next step.

Petrolhead — a motorcycle training brand run by William Shaker — needed a high-converting opt-in page to grow their email list and give away their premium Motorcycle Mastery guide for free.

The challenge was making a free guide feel valuable enough that visitors would eagerly opt in. The goal was a full lead magnet funnel built inside GoHighLevel — leading with a compelling headline, showcasing the guide’s content through chapter previews, and stacking social proof to drive maximum opt-ins.

What the funnel needed to do

Make the guide tangible, preview its real learning content, establish authority, remove the reasons someone might hesitate, and connect every opt-in to automated lead delivery.

What I built

A lead magnet funnel that puts proof before the opt-in.

The original GoHighLevel funnel makes the guide’s content, value, authority, and next step visible throughout the page so the offer feels credible before a visitor enters their details.

01 · FIRST IMPRESSION

Hero Section

“Ride Like a Pro: Master Motorcycle Skills in Just 90 Days!” — bold headline with book mockup image and a prominent “Download the FREE Motorcycle Mastery Guide” CTA button above the fold.

02 · CONTENT PREVIEW

6 Chapter Previews

“Discover What Awaits in Motorcycle Mastery” — all 6 chapters displayed with real images and descriptions: Foundation for Success, Conquer the Basics, Street-Smart Survival Skills, Psychology of Riding, Level Up Your Riding Game, and Beyond the Basics.

03 · AUTHORITY SIGNAL

Social Proof Stats

Three credibility numbers displayed prominently — 38K Subscribers, 5+ Years of Riding, 12+ Advanced Courses Completed — to establish instant authority.

04 · VALUE CLARITY

Value Stack Section

“Ready to Transform Your Riding Journey?” — 120-page guide breakdown with bullet points covering skills, mindset, real-world challenges, and safety — making the free offer feel like a no-brainer.

05 · HESITATION REMOVAL

Why Grab This Free

“Why Grab This $97 Guide for Free?” — three bold reasons: First 500 downloads are free, Expert insights for every level, and Safety first. Stacked objection removal before the CTA.

06 · RIDER PROOF

Real Testimonials

“Real Riders, Real Results!” — real social media screenshots from verified riders showing the guide’s impact — authentic social proof rather than generic quotes.

07 · HUMAN CONNECTION

Author Bio

Personal section from William Shaker — his riding story, why he created the guide, and a personal invitation to take the first step — building a human connection before the final CTA.

08 · FINAL OBJECTIONS

FAQ Section

7 pre-answered questions — Who is this for, Is it really free, What will I learn, Do I need a motorcycle, How do I get it, Is it for advanced riders, Why should I trust you — removing every last objection.

Tools & integrations
