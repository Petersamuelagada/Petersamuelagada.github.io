The project

Clear pricing without making the choice harder.

Digital Lead Marketing needed a dedicated pricing page to present two distinct product types — a CRM software subscription and a done-for-you ad campaign management service — to business owners ready to scale their marketing.

The page had to feel transparent and fair while guiding visitors toward the right plan for their budget and goals. The full page was built inside GoHighLevel with a cream and amber design, Monthly/Yearly CRM toggle, three-tier service comparison, and detailed business-marketing explanation.

The primary conversion job was to remove pricing uncertainty: explain the software route, compare the service route, then make a conversation feel like the natural next step.

What I built

One pricing system for clarity and qualified action.

The original build sets out the DLM software entry route and service-management options in a way that lets prospective buyers self-select before they enquire.

Hero headline

“Choose the Plan That Powers Your Growth” with confirmation that plans include DLM Lead Connector System, automation tools, and campaign management.

Monthly / Yearly toggle

Visitors self-select a preferred billing cycle and see the DLM Lead Connector Software pricing immediately.

DLM Lead Connector

Featured CRM card: 60 Day FREE Trial → $149/mo after the trial period, creating a simple low-risk entry route.

Three service tiers

Standard ($999 setup + $399/mo tech + $1,999/mo management), Premium ($1,499 setup + $3,499/mo), and Professional ($999 setup + $2,999/mo).

Pricing philosophy

Transparency-led copy explaining project-based and hourly options, with no two campaigns treated as identical.

Campaign CTA

A final Marketing Campaigns action follows the full price context, reducing hesitation before an enquiry.
