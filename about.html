My story

I fix what others left half-built.

My process

How I work.

I don’t jump straight into building. The first thing I do is listen.

01

Discovery & listening

I want to understand your offer, how you close clients, where leads are coming from, and what’s actually breaking down in your process. Most of the time business owners realize the problem runs deeper than they thought—and that’s fine.

→
02

Map & plan

Once I understand the full picture I map everything out, get your sign-off, and then I build it. Every workflow. Every automation. Every page. Every integration.

→
03

Build & test

Before I hand anything over I test it end to end myself—because the last thing I want is to deliver something that breaks the moment a real lead touches it.

→
04

Handover & support

I’m not the type to disappear after handover. If something isn’t working the way it should, I want to know about it. Your success is my reputation.
