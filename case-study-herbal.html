The project

A product launch needs a website that feels ready to trust.

A premium herbal health brand needed a full website to launch their new product line. They had a great product but no online presence — no website, no lead capture, and no automated follow-up system.

They needed a site that looked professional, trustworthy, and conversion-focused — designed to turn visitors into buyers and leads into customers.

What the system needed to do

Give visitors clear product information, support buying confidence with proof and FAQ content, capture interested visitors, and connect those contacts to HubSpot CRM.
