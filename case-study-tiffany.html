The project

Make a complex lending offer feel more human, clear, and trusted.

Tiffany Dawson — a Senior Loan Officer based in Irvine, California — needed a landing page that could communicate her personal mission clearly: helping families who have been told “no” by traditional lenders find a path to homeownership and generational wealth.

The page needed to present both qualified and non-qualified mortgage products, build personal trust through her story and video testimonials, and drive free loan consultation bookings — all with an AI chatbot handling instant visitor questions.

I built the full page inside GoHighLevel — black and gold luxury aesthetic, video-led hero, dual mortgage product comparison, founder bio, comprehensive FAQ, and a strong pre-qualification CTA.

What the page needed to do

Turn a visitor’s uncertainty into clarity with clear product categories, real-story proof, direct answers, and a visible consultation route, without overwhelming them.

What I built

A mortgage page that moves from mission to clarity to action.

The original GoHighLevel page makes trust visible in several forms: a founder-led story, video testimonial proof, product comparison, direct answers, AI chat, and low-friction consultation paths.

01 · FIRST CONNECTION

Hero Section with AI Chatbot

“Unlock The Keys To Homeownership & Generational Wealth” — video-led hero with Tiffany front and centre, plus an AI chatbot overlay for instant visitor Q&A without interrupting the page flow.

02 · FAMILY PROOF

Video Social Proof Gallery

Multiple family video testimonials showing real clients who were approved and moved into their dream homes — the most powerful trust-builder on the page, placed before the product section.

03 · PRODUCT CLARITY

Mortgage Product Comparison

Side-by-side breakdown of Qualified Mortgage Products (FHA, VA, Conventional, USDA, Down Payment Assistance) vs Non-Qualified Products (Bank Statement, Asset Depletion, DSCR, Hard Money, etc.).

04 · MILESTONE PROOF

Family Success Story

“Another Family Approved & Moving Into Their Dream Home!” — real client milestone with photo, reinforcing that Tiffany’s guidance turns homeownership dreams into reality.

05 · FOUNDER TRUST

About Tiffany Dawson

Personal story section — started 2022, Senior Loan Officer, Irvine CA, real estate investor background. Humanises the brand and builds trust before the final CTA.

06 · ANSWER LAYER

Comprehensive FAQ

8+ questions covering: states she lends in, what a lender does, broker benefits, prequalification vs preapproval, documents needed, closing process, credit repair, and credit responsibilities.

07 · FINAL DIRECTION

Pre-Qualification CTA

“Your Path To Homeownership Is Closer Than You Think!” — final section with “Start My Pre-Qualification Now!” button driving conversions after all objections have been addressed.

08 · CONSULTATION ROUTES

Free Loan Consultation CTA

Multiple “Start My Free Loan Consultation” CTAs placed strategically throughout the page — reducing friction and capturing leads at every stage of the visitor journey.

Tools & integrations

Built for trust-led mortgage conversations and consultation conversion.

The original implementation combines GoHighLevel with AI chat, video testimonials, mortgage product tables, consultation booking, FAQ content, premium design, and mobile-ready presentation.
