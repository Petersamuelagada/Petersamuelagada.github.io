The project

Give an emerging offer the clarity to generate leads.

Precision AI Growth needed a professional landing page to position their AI marketing services and generate leads from businesses — particularly in the home services and restoration niche.

They had no digital presence to communicate their offer clearly. The goal was a high-converting landing page built inside GoHighLevel — leading with a bold 24/7 AI answering agent offer, showcasing their core AI marketing services, and building trust with Google-verified client testimonials.

What the page needed to do

Make the AI answering-agent promise immediate, reveal the wider service depth, answer adoption questions, and build the credibility needed for a business to enquire.

A landing page with the AI offer and trust proof in sequence.
