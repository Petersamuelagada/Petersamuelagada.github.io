The Project

Pro Studio is a Toronto-based real estate media company serving top agents across the GTA. They needed a premium website that matched the quality of their work — architectural in feel, dark and luxurious in design, and structured to convert agents into booking clients.

The site needed to clearly present six distinct media services, justify premium pricing through quality proof, showcase real listing results, and make booking a shoot as frictionless as possible — all while serving Toronto and surrounding areas.

I built the full website inside GoHighLevel — dark gold aesthetic, comprehensive service grid, curated packages, real listing showcase, and a clean "How It Works" section closing with a booking form.

What I Built

🏠 Hero Section

"Architectural-Grade Real Estate Media That Sells Listings Fast." — bold headline with service tags (Photo · 4K Video · 3D Matterport · Drone · Social Reels) and dual CTAs: Book a Shoot and WhatsApp call button.

✅ Why Top Agents Choose Us

Four trust pillars: Cinematic Quality, 24-Hour Turnaround, All-In-One Arsenal, and a fifth proof bar showing agent ratings, blogTO feature, properties shot, and listings captured.

📸 Comprehensive Media Services

6-service grid: Real Estate Photography, 4K Cinematic Video, 3D Matterport Tours, Drone Aerials, Social Media Reels, and Detailed Floor Plans — each with its own visual and description.

📦 Curated Packages Section

Tiered listing packages — Essential, Premium, Signature, and Expert Support — with clear pricing and feature breakdowns, making it easy for agents to select the right package for their listing.

🏆 Proven Results, Real Closings

Real listing showcase with before/after property photos and sale data — demonstrating that Pro Studio's media directly contributes to faster sales and higher closing prices.

📱 Premium Social Media Reels

Dedicated section for vertical video reels optimised for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook — showing agents exactly how their listings will look on social media.

🎬 4K Cinematic Video & 3D Matterport

Separate deep-dive sections for the two flagship services — walkthroughs, immersive 3D tours, and the tech behind them, building confidence in the quality of the deliverable.

🗺️ How It Works + FAQ + Booking Form

3-step process (Book → We Capture → Receive in 24hrs), FAQ section addressing agent objections, and a final booking form — all designed to close the decision on the page.
