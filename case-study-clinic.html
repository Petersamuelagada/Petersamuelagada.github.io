The problem

Trust before the first appointment.

A health and wellness clinic was operating without a proper online presence. Potential patients were searching for their services online but could not find them — or landed on an outdated, unprofessional page that did not build trust.

They needed a complete website that looked credible, professional, and converted visitors into booked appointments, with automatic follow-up so no lead slipped through the cracks.

The goal was a clear patient journey: understand services, meet the team, see social proof, submit an enquiry, then receive a timely, useful follow-up sequence.

The solution

A complete clinic experience, connected to follow-up.

The original build combines credibility, education, service discovery, and lead capture in one site, then moves enquiries into GoHighLevel automation.

Professional hero

“Fueling Your Potential With Nutrition” with clear CTA, professional photography, and above-the-fold trust elements.

Services showcase

Massage therapy, neuromuscular therapy, restorative exercises, clear descriptions, and appointment CTAs.

Team profiles

Photos, qualifications, and specialities create a personal connection and build confidence with potential patients.

Testimonials & proof

Real patient testimonials, star ratings, and partner logos are structured to reduce doubt and build credibility.

Contact + GHL

Lead capture connects directly to GoHighLevel CRM, triggering automatic email and SMS follow-up within 60 seconds.

Five-day follow-up

Welcome message, service information, testimonial, FAQ, and final booking reminder keep new enquiries engaged.

Before vs. after
