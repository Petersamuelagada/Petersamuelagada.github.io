The project

Make a course decision feel clear and worthwhile.

Scott Spencer is a photography educator who needed a professional course landing page that could do the selling for him — clearly communicate the value of his course, build trust with potential students, and convert visitors into paying enrollees.

The challenge was creating a page that felt premium enough to justify 3-tier pricing ($69/$99/$179), while making the decision to enroll feel easy and obvious for beginners and intermediate photographers alike.

I built the entire page inside GoHighLevel — from hero to final CTA — with a full sales structure designed around the student journey.

What the page needed to do

Establish instructor authority, preview the learning journey, make each course tier tangible, answer common concerns, and move interested visitors to a straightforward enrollment action.

What I built

A course page structured around the student journey.

The original GoHighLevel launch page creates a complete sales story: clarify the outcome, build authority, explain the course, show student proof, make pricing comparable, and provide a clean next step.

01 · CLEAR OUTCOME

Hero Section

Bold headline with a clear value proposition — immediately communicates who the course is for and what they’ll achieve.

02 · AUTHORITY

Instructor Bio

Scott Spencer’s background, credentials, and teaching philosophy — builds authority and trust before the ask.

03 · LEARNING PATH

Course Overview

6-module breakdown showing exactly what students learn — structured to handle objections and build excitement.

04 · TANGIBLE VALUE

What You’ll Get

Clear bullet list of deliverables, resources, and bonuses — makes the value of each tier feel concrete and tangible.

05 · STUDENT PROOF

Student Testimonials

Real social proof from enrolled students — placed strategically before the pricing section to reduce hesitation.

06 · ENROLLMENT CHOICE

3-Tier Pricing

Basic ($69) / Standard ($99) / Premium ($179) — clearly differentiated with feature lists and a highlighted recommended tier.

07 · OBJECTION HANDLING

FAQ Section

Addressed the most common objections — “Is this for beginners?”, “What equipment do I need?”, “Is there a refund policy?”

08 · FINAL ACTION

Enrollment CTA

Final call-to-action section with urgency copy and a direct enroll button — no friction, no confusion.

Tools & integrations

Built for a course launch that converts interest.

The original implementation combines a GoHighLevel sales page with custom design, course-tier presentation, email automation, capture forms, responsive design, conversion layout, and direct calls to action.
