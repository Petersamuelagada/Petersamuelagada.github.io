Case Study

Mastering Photography Course Landing Page

High-converting enrollment system for Scott Spencer's Photography Course.

The Challenge

The client needed a professional, high-converting landing page to launch their "Mastering Photography" course. The goal was to handle 1,000+ students with a clear 3-tier pricing structure and a seamless enrollment flow.

1,000+ Students Enrolled
300+ 5-Star Reviews
99% Satisfaction Rate

The Solution

Built entirely within GoHighLevel, I designed a multi-section landing page featuring:

  • Dynamic Hero Section with clear Value Proposition.
  • 6-Module Course Breakdown with interactive elements.
  • 3-Tier Pricing Table ($69 / $99 / $179) with direct checkout links.
  • Social Proof section featuring automated testimonial sliders.
