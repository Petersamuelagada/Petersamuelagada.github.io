FAQ

Questions clients ask before hiring me.

Clear expectations matter. Here are the most common questions before a system build begins.

How long does a typical project take?

A single landing page usually takes 3–5 days. A full GHL buildout with automations and email sequences typically takes 7–14 days. Larger systems with integrations and AI can take 2–4 weeks. A clear timeline is agreed before work begins.

Do you work across different time zones?

Yes. Most clients are in the US, UK, and Canada. Communication is planned around a shared working window, with clear updates and a Calendly link for booking calls.

Do I need to already have GoHighLevel?

No. I can set up a new GHL account from scratch or work inside your existing account. A discovery call can help determine whether GHL is the right fit before you commit.

What do you need from me to get started?

Usually access to the relevant system, a clear brief on the business and goals, and any branding assets you already have. The discovery process is designed to surface the important questions upfront.

Do you offer revisions and support?

Yes. Projects include revisions until the agreed result is reached, followed by testing and post-delivery support to help the system run reliably.
