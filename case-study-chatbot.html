The project

A directory listing that can start the right conversation.

The client operated a business directory where multiple local businesses were listed and visitors browsed to find services. The problem was conversion — visitors arrived, read a listing, and left. There was no interactive element to capture interest, qualify leads, or route enquiries to the right business in real time.

Each business listing now gets its own AI assistant trained on that specific business's services, FAQs, pricing, and unique selling points. The chatbot engages visitors, qualifies them through natural conversation, captures contact details, and feeds them into the GHL CRM pipeline automatically.

The supplied build turns a static contact route into a structured flow: service discovery, need assessment, lead capture, business notification, and optional appointment booking.
Chatbot evidence · directory business assistant

What it handles

Trained per business. Connected to the next step.

The original solution combines tailored business knowledge with lead qualification and GoHighLevel routing so a listing can respond with the right information instead of a generic form.

Custom training

Each chatbot is trained on the specific business's services, FAQs, pricing, and objection handling so answers are accurate rather than generic.

Instant lead capture

The bot naturally collects name, email, phone, and qualifying details through conversation, then sends leads into GHL CRM.

Smart qualification

It asks about service needs, timing, and budget before handoff so only qualified leads go to the business.

Appointment booking

For connected calendars, the bot offers and confirms appointments directly in the conversation.

Business notification

Captured leads trigger SMS and email notification with details so the business can follow up while interest is warm.

Conversation improvement

As conversations happen, the AI refines its responses to improve qualification accuracy and lead quality over time.

Knowledge base & onboarding

Services, pricing, FAQs, objection handling scripts, and unique selling points become each business's AI knowledge base.

AI model & personality

The assistant is trained to match the business tone, from professional law firms to friendly home services and authoritative medical clinics.

Conversation flow design

Greeting, discovery, need assessment, lead capture, and handoff all have fallback responses for unexpected inputs.

GHL CRM integration

Every captured lead creates a contact, applies tags, assigns the correct pipeline stage, and triggers follow-up automatically.

Listing widget embed

A mobile-optimised, fast-loading chat widget is embedded on each business listing page.

Testing & QA

The supplied build was tested across 50+ conversation scenarios, including edge cases, objections, and off-topic inputs.
