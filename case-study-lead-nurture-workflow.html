The Project

The client was generating inbound leads from multiple sources — opt-in forms, calendar bookings, and direct replies — but had no consistent follow-up system. Speed-to-lead was slow, and by the time the team reached out manually, prospects had already moved on or forgotten why they enquired.

There was no differentiation between lead sources either. Someone who had just booked an appointment was receiving the same generic follow-up as someone who only filled out a form. The result was low show-up rates, poor engagement, and missed revenue. I built a multi-trigger nurture workflow that responds to every lead immediately — and keeps nurturing intelligently until they either engage or opt out.

GHL workflow canvas — multi-trigger SMS, Email & Video nurture sequence with smart branching

How It Works

⚡ 3 Trigger Points

📋
Form Submit
Any opt-in or lead capture form submission instantly triggers the workflow — response within 60 seconds of the lead coming in.
📅
Appointment Booking
Calendar bookings trigger a parallel confirmation + reminder sequence with messaging specific to booked prospects.
💬
Lead Reply
When a lead replies to any outreach, they're routed to a conversation branch — stopping automated messages immediately.

📅 The Full Sequence

  • 1
    Immediate — Within 60 Seconds
    Welcome SMS Sent
    The moment a lead enters, they receive a personalised SMS using their first name, referencing what they opted in for, and a direct reply CTA. Achieves instant speed-to-lead.
    SMSFirst Name PersonalisedReply CTA
  • 2
    5 Minutes After SMS
    Welcome Email Delivered
    A branded welcome email follows — expanding on the value proposition, social proof, and a clear next step (book a call, watch the video, reply with questions). Mobile-first design.
    EmailBranded TemplateSocial ProofCTA Button
  • ?
    Wait — Check for Reply
    Smart Reply Detection
    Before sending anything else, the system checks: has the lead replied? If yes — stop and route to human handoff. If no — continue. Prevents automated messages interrupting live conversations.
    If/Else BranchReply DetectionHuman Handoff
  • 3
    Day 2 — 24 Hours Later
    Video Follow-Up Email
    If no reply and no booking, a video email fires. Features a personalised Loom or VSL thumbnail linking to a short video — client testimonial, demo, or message from the owner. Video dramatically increases click rates.
    EmailVideo EmbedLoom / VSLClick Tracking
  • 4
    Day 3 — Follow-Up SMS
    Soft Check-In SMS
    A casual, conversational SMS asking if they had a chance to watch the video. Includes a direct booking link. This message has the highest reply rate in the sequence because it feels human, not automated.
    SMSConversational ToneBooking Link
  • 5
    Day 5 — Final Touch
    Last Chance Email + Smart End
    Final email with a clear subject line ("Still interested?") and two-option CTA: book now or reply to opt out. If no response → moved to 30-day re-engagement list. If books → appointment sequence starts.
