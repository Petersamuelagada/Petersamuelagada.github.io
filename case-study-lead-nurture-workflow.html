The project

Every inbound signal gets the right next move.

The client was generating inbound leads from multiple sources — opt-in forms, calendar bookings, and direct replies — but had no consistent follow-up system. Speed-to-lead was slow, and by the time the team reached out manually, prospects had already moved on or forgotten why they enquired.

There was no differentiation between lead sources either. Someone who had just booked an appointment was receiving the same generic follow-up as someone who only filled out a form. The result was low show-up rates, poor engagement, and missed revenue. I built a multi-trigger nurture workflow that responds to every lead immediately — and keeps nurturing intelligently until they either engage or opt out.

What the system needed to do

Start each prospect on the appropriate message route, reply at speed, protect live conversations from automated messages, and continue follow-up only when it is appropriate.

Three entry routes

Same standard of response. Different starting context.

The original workflow makes lead source part of the logic. It is built to respond quickly without treating form submissions, appointment bookings, and live replies as identical events.

The full sequence

Six deliberate touches — with room for a real reply.

Each original stage has a timing rule, purpose, and smart stop. The sequence gives leads a clear path forward without sending automated messages over a live conversation.

01Immediate · within 60 seconds

Welcome SMS Sent

The moment a lead enters, they receive a personalised SMS using their first name, referencing what they opted in for, and a direct reply CTA. Achieves instant speed-to-lead.

Smart Reply Detection

Before sending anything else, the system checks: has the lead replied? If yes — stop and route to human handoff. If no — continue. Prevents automated messages interrupting live conversations.

Video Follow-Up Email

If no reply and no booking, a video email fires. Features a personalised Loom or VSL thumbnail linking to a short video — client testimonial, demo, or message from the owner. Video dramatically increases click rates.
