Lead Nurture Workflow — SMS, Email & Video

A multi-trigger GHL nurture workflow that fires on form submit, appointment booking, or lead reply. It sends a welcome SMS and email, checks for reply, then follows with video when no response; smart branching ends the sequence when a lead engages.

System brief

Respond instantly to every inbound lead, tailor the start route to context, and keep human conversations free from unwanted automation.

Project focus

Improve speed-to-lead and follow-up consistency without treating booked prospects, form leads, and replies as the same signal.

The project

Turn every inbound signal into the right next move.

The client generated inbound leads through opt-in forms, calendar bookings, and direct replies but had no consistent follow-up system. By the time the team responded manually, prospects had moved on or forgotten why they enquired.

The workflow responds to each lead immediately and keeps nurturing intelligently until they engage or opt out, with different behaviour for booked prospects and simple form leads.

The system's key discipline is its stop logic: a lead reply routes to conversation and halts automated messages, so real human engagement is not interrupted.
Workflow evidence · GHL multi-trigger nurture canvas

Three entry routes

Same standard of response. Different starting context.

📋 Form submit

Any opt-in or lead-capture form instantly triggers the workflow, targeting a response within 60 seconds.

📅 Appointment booking

Calendar bookings begin a parallel confirmation and reminder sequence for booked prospects.

💬 Lead reply

When a lead replies, the conversation branch takes over and automated messages stop immediately.

The original nurture route

Five deliberate touches — with room for a real reply.

Immediate · within 60 seconds

Welcome SMS

Personalised first-name SMS referencing the opt-in and asking for a direct reply.

Five minutes later

Welcome email

Branded email expands the value proposition, social proof, and next step.

Wait and check

Smart reply detection

If the lead replied, stop and route to human handoff. If not, continue the sequence.

Day 2 · 24 hours later

Video follow-up

Video email carries a personalised Loom/VSL thumbnail to a testimonial, demo, or owner message.

Day 3

Soft check-in SMS

Conversational check-in asks whether they watched the video and includes a booking link.

Day 5

Last chance + smart end

Final email offers book-now or opt-out actions; no response moves to a 30-day re-engagement list, while a booking starts appointment automation.
