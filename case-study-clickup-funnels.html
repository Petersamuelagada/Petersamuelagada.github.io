The project

A clear path from coaching offer to funnel system.

ClickUp Funnels needed a sales page that could speak directly to coaches and course creators who were overwhelmed by tech, unsure where to start, and watching competitors with simpler systems outperform them.

The page positions their DFY Sales Funnel System as a complete pipeline built from the ground up, with copy written, funnels designed, and optimisation after launch. The build leads with the promise, surfaces the real pain points, explains the components, and closes with a clear “Here's How It Works” path and free funnel audit CTA.

The supplied work is designed to explain what is included, show the process clearly, and make the next step feel simple for visitors who do not want to manage the technical build themselves.
