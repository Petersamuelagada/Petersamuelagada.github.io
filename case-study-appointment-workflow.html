Appointment Status Update Workflow — 6-Branch Pipeline Automation

Built a multi-branch GHL workflow handling 6 appointment outcomes — Unable to Contact, Contacted, No Show, Showed, Sold, and Unqualified. Each branch auto-updates the pipeline stage, tags the contact, sends Thank You email + SMS, requests reviews, and nurtures leads. Zero manual work after setup.

System brief

One appointment status update routes each contact into the correct follow-up sequence, pipeline stage, tag, and message path.

Project focus

Keep the pipeline honest and make every outcome actionable without asking the sales team to chase admin work.

The project

Every appointment outcome gets the right next step.

The client was managing a high-volume appointment business and spending hours every day manually updating CRM stages, sending follow-up messages, and tracking lead outcomes.

With no automation in place, leads were falling through the cracks. No-shows weren't being re-engaged. Sold contacts weren't receiving onboarding. The sales team was wasting time on admin instead of selling. I was brought in to build a system that handles every outcome automatically — the moment an appointment status is updated in GHL, the right sequence fires immediately.

What the system needed to do

Recognise the appointment result, move the contact to the right pipeline stage, apply the right tag, and send only the appropriate SMS and email follow-up.

Workflow evidence · 6-branch appointment automation

How it works

Six outcomes. Six controlled paths.

Each original branch owns its pipeline move, messages, tags, and timing. That means one status update creates the correct next action without duplicate or conflicting follow-up.

BRANCH 01

Unable to Contact

  • Tag: Unable to Contact
  • Move to Re-Engage stage
  • SMS + 24hr follow-up email
  • Auto-retry call task
BRANCH 02

Contacted

  • Tag: Contacted
  • Move to Appointment Set
  • Confirmation SMS + Email
  • 24hr + 1hr reminders
BRANCH 03

No Show

  • Tag: No Show
  • Move to No Show stage
  • Reschedule email
  • 3-day re-engagement drip
BRANCH 04

Showed

  • Tag: Showed – Not Sold
  • Move to Showed stage
  • Thank you SMS
  • Nurture sequence starts
BRANCH 05

Sold ✓

  • Tag: Client – Sold
  • Move to Won stage
  • Welcome SMS + onboarding
  • Review + referral requests
BRANCH 06

Unqualified

  • Tag: Unqualified
  • Move to Lost stage
  • Remove active sequences
  • 90-day re-engage list

Trigger: appointment status changes

The workflow fires automatically the moment a GHL appointment status is updated — whether by a sales rep, calendar event, or inbound action.

Branch logic routes the contact

A multi-condition branch reads the outcome and sends the contact down one of 6 fully independent paths.

Pipeline stages auto-update

No Show moves to No Show, Sold moves to Won, and Unqualified moves to Lost. The pipeline reflects reality in real time.

Tags apply for segmentation

Every contact gets the appropriate tag so future campaigns, filters, and automations target correctly.

SMS + email send automatically

Each branch triggers personalised messages timed to its outcome, from reminders to onboarding and review requests.

Smart stops prevent over-communication

If a contact progresses from No Show to Sold, the system detects the new status and stops the previous sequence.

Tools & stack

One workflow connected to the whole appointment system.
