The Project

The client operated a business directory platform where multiple local businesses were listed and visitors browsed to find services. The problem was conversion — visitors arrived, read a listing, and left. There was no interactive element to capture interest, qualify leads, or route enquiries to the right business in real time.

Most directory platforms rely on a static "Contact Us" form — but modern visitors want immediate, personalised responses. If they have to wait for a human to reply, they move on. The client needed every business listing to feel like it had a dedicated, knowledgeable representative available at any time of day or night.

AI chatbot mockup — custom-trained business assistant for directory platform listings

How It Works

Each business listing on the directory gets its own AI assistant — custom-trained on that specific business's services, FAQs, pricing, and unique selling points. The chatbot engages visitors instantly, qualifies them through natural conversation, captures their contact details, and feeds them directly into the GHL CRM pipeline — automatically.

🔑 Key Features Built

🧠
Custom Training Per Business
Each chatbot is trained on that specific business's services, FAQs, pricing, and objection handling — so it answers accurately, not generically.
⚡
Instant Lead Capture
The bot naturally collects name, email, phone, and qualifying details through conversation — no clunky forms. All leads push straight into GHL CRM.
🔀
Smart Qualification Flow
Asks the right questions to qualify the lead — service needed, timeline, budget — before passing to the business. Only qualified leads get through.
📅
Appointment Booking Integration
For businesses with calendars connected, the bot offers and confirms appointments directly in the conversation — no redirects, no friction.
🔔
Instant Business Notification
The moment a lead is captured, the business gets an SMS and email notification with all details — so they can follow up while the lead is still warm.
📈
Gets Smarter Over Time
As more conversations happen, the AI refines its responses — improving qualification accuracy and lead quality automatically over time.

⚙️ How It Was Built

  • 📋
    Business Onboarding & Knowledge Base
    For each business listing, I created a structured knowledge base — services, pricing, FAQs, objection handling scripts, and unique selling points. This becomes the AI's "brain" for that business.
  • 🤖
    AI Model Training & Personality
    The AI was trained to match each business's tone — professional for law firms, friendly for home services, authoritative for medical clinics. Each bot feels like a real brand representative.
  • 🔀
    Conversation Flow Design
    I designed the qualification flow — greeting, service discovery, need assessment, lead capture, and handoff. Each stage has fallback responses for unexpected inputs so the bot never gets stuck.
  • 🔗
    GHL CRM Integration
    Every captured lead is pushed into GoHighLevel — creating a contact, applying the right tags, assigning to the correct pipeline stage, and triggering the follow-up workflow automatically.
  • 💬
    Widget Embed on Directory Listings
    A lightweight chat widget was embedded on each business's listing page — mobile-optimised, fast-loading, and styled to match the directory's branding. Zero page speed impact.
  • ✅
    Testing, QA & Launch
    Full QA testing across 50+ conversation scenarios — edge cases, off-topic inputs, competitor mentions, pricing objections. Every failure point addressed before going live.
