The Project

Blue Hawk Automation needed a complete website that could clearly explain algorithmic trading to a non-technical audience, position their approach as superior to traditional income strategies, and convert visitors into members through a strong CTA and contact form.

The site needed to establish credibility through founder story, case studies, client testimonials, and a clear breakdown of their two service offerings — License access and Funded Account partnerships.

I built the full site inside GoHighLevel — dark navy aesthetic with blue accents, comparison-style opportunity section, case study showcase, team profiles, and a community CTA closing the page.

What I Built

🚀 Hero Section

"Blue Hawk Automation Unleash The Full Power Of Algorithmic Trading Strategies" — bold headline with subtext on maximising precision, minimising risks, and achieving consistent results.

👤 About Dave Nelson

Founder story section — 14+ years forex experience, originally from Australia, built for credibility with individual and institutional clients. Positions Dave as the trusted expert.

⚖️ Why This Is the Best Opportunity

5-way comparison grid: Trading Yourself, Real Estate, Index/Mutual Funds, Traditional Business/Franchise, and AI Trading — showing why algorithmic trading wins on every dimension.

🛠️ Blue Hawk Services

Two clear tracks: License (algorithm access, no split profit, account balance funding) and Funded Account (partner with Blue Hawk, completely automated). Each with its own CTA.

📊 Case Studies Section

"Unlock Success: Download Our Case Studies" — real performance data, analysis of proven trading strategies, market trend insights, and actionable tips to enhance trading performance.

⭐ Client Testimonials

"See Why Our Clients Are Loving Our Algorithm" — carousel of real client reviews with names, proving the algo delivers consistent passive income results.

👥 Meet Our Team

Three team profiles: Dave Nelson (Co-Founder/CEO), Juan Mancera (Co-Founder/COO), and Cesar Menacho (Head of Client Support) — building human trust and accountability.

📬 Contact & Community CTA

"Let's Get Started To Become A Part Of BlueHawk Community Today" — contact form with phone, email, and message fields plus FAQ section addressing the top investment objections.
