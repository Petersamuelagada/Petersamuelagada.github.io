The project

Complex financial ideas made clear and credible.

Blue Hawk Automation needed a complete website that could clearly explain algorithmic trading to a non-technical audience, position their approach against traditional income strategies, and convert visitors into members through a strong CTA and contact form.

The site needed to establish credibility through founder story, case studies, client testimonials, and a clear breakdown of License access and Funded Account partnerships. I built the full site inside GoHighLevel with a dark navy aesthetic, comparison-style opportunity section, case-study showcase, team profiles, and a community CTA.

The goal was not to make financial promises. It was to give visitors a structured way to understand Blue Hawk's offer, team, education, access models, and next step.

What I built

One platform for trust, education, and action.

The original build combined the following site components to communicate the offer and give prospects a route into the Blue Hawk community.

Hero & positioning

“Blue Hawk Automation Unleash The Full Power Of Algorithmic Trading Strategies” with precision, risk, and consistency messaging.

Founder authority

Dave Nelson's story, 14+ years of forex experience, and context for individual and institutional clients.

Opportunity comparison

A five-way comparison covering Trading Yourself, Real Estate, Index/Mutual Funds, Traditional Business/Franchise, and AI Trading.

Two service tracks

License access and Funded Account partnerships, each with its own explanation and CTA.

Case-study showcase

“Unlock Success: Download Our Case Studies” with performance data, analysis, strategy context, and market insights.

Testimonials & team

Client reviews plus three profiles: Dave Nelson, Juan Mancera, and Cesar Menacho to build accountability.

Community CTA

“Let's Get Started To Become A Part Of BlueHawk Community Today” with contact form, FAQ, phone, email, and message fields.

Conversion structure

A mobile-optimised layout built inside GoHighLevel to move visitors from offer understanding to contact action.
